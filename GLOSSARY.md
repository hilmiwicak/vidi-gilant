# Vidi

A birthday scavenger hunt played on her phone. She travels to places that are significant to the couple, solves a short puzzle at each one, and ends at a planned dinner.

## Language

**Hunt**:
The whole birthday experience, from the Welcome to the dinner.
_Avoid_: Game, quest, tour, route, treasure hunt

**Welcome**:
The opening sequence before the first Stop: permissions, a birthday greeting, and the Mission intro. It has no place to reach, so it is not a Stop.
_Avoid_: Stop 0, intro, onboarding

**Stage**:
One tap-to-advance frame inside a screen, shown on its own before the next one. The Welcome, the Mission intro, the last-Stop congratulation and the final screen are each a sequence of Stages. Not to be confused with a Stop, which is a place.
_Avoid_: Step, beat, slide, page

**Plan**:
One of two versions of the Hunt. He picks it before the Welcome and it never changes mid-Hunt. Plan A runs from late morning, covers Bintaro and Jakarta, includes the Mission, and ends with dinner around 5 to 6pm. Plan B starts after he picks her up from work in Jakarta, stays in Jakarta, makes the Mission optional, and ends with dinner around 8pm.
_Avoid_: Route, mode, variant

**Stop**:
A significant place she must physically reach. The last Stop of every Plan is the dinner. Plan A has 2 to 3 Stops around Bintaro, 0 to 2 in Jakarta, then the dinner. Plan B has 2 Stops close together in Jakarta, then the dinner.
_Avoid_: Checkpoint, location, waypoint, station

**Puzzle**:
A short, gentle question asking where a Stop is. Each Stop owns the Puzzle that leads to it, and she sees it while travelling there. It is never graded: she can only think about it, and it must never put her in a bad mood. It may be a simple personal question.
_Avoid_: Riddle, quiz, task

**Reveal**:
The piece of content she receives at a Stop: a voice message, video, song, or text message. Formats vary from Stop to Stop. It never blocks progress: she can open it at any time after Arrival, including while travelling to the next Stop.
_Avoid_: Reward, content, unlock

**Clue**:
An easier hint for a Puzzle. The button that shows it is always available, she presses it whenever she wants, and nothing counts against her.
_Avoid_: Hint, riddle

**Mission**:
The ongoing task that runs through the entire Hunt: she hands out 10 to 20 prepared food boxes to people along the way.
_Avoid_: Side quest, charity, giveaway

## Finding a Stop

**Pointer**:
The arrow and distance-to-target display shown while she heads to a Stop, in the style of Actionbound's "Find spot" screen.
_Avoid_: Compass, radar, tracker

**Arrival**:
The moment the Hunt accepts that she has reached a Stop. It happens automatically when she is close enough, or through a Fallback.
_Avoid_: Check-in, unlock, completion

**Fallback**:
Any way to reach Arrival when automatic detection fails. Fallbacks are tried in a fixed order: Photo check, Outline photo, Code, then Admin mode.
_Avoid_: Backup, override, bypass

**Photo check**:
A photo she takes at the Stop, compared by an AI model against a reference photo of the place.
_Avoid_: Photo verification, image match

**Outline photo**:
A Fallback where she photographs a distinctive shape at the Stop (e.g. a roofline and window) that matches an outline shown in the app. It is accepted without any verification.
_Avoid_: Silhouette, sketch

**Code**:
A short secret tied to one Stop. He tells it to her if every other Fallback fails.
_Avoid_: Coupon, referral code, PIN, password

**Master code**:
A Code that opens Admin mode on her phone when typed into the Code field, as an alternative to the hidden tap gesture.
_Avoid_: Backdoor, admin code

**Event log**:
A record of Hunt progress and errors (Stop reached, Fallback used, Photo check result), kept for 30 days. It never contains her location, accuracy or distance.
_Avoid_: Tracking, analytics, telemetry

**Boxes counter**:
The tally of food boxes she has handed out for the Mission. She can add or subtract one at a time, and it never affects Stop progression.
_Avoid_: Score, points, progress

**Admin mode**:
A hidden facility on her own device that lets him skip or reset Stops if the Hunt goes wrong. She is never meant to see it.
_Avoid_: Test mode, debug mode, cheat
